### Three measured facts about this file, and what they cost

None of the three is a licence or provenance problem, and none of them is repaired by the commit that imported this file. The file is preserved unmodified as the source of record, exactly as `virgil-model-candidate-01.glb` was. They are recorded here because they are why this file is a candidate and not usable as it stands.

**1. No rig and no animations.** The owner reported being able to animate in Meshy, but this export contains neither a skin nor an animation: both arrays are absent, and there is no joint hierarchy. Any animated use of this model needs a fresh export from Meshy that carries them, or a rig built here. Nothing in this file can be animated by posing it.

**2. The texture payload alone consumes or exceeds the transfer budget for the whole slice.** `docs/architecture/PERFORMANCE_STRATEGY.md` sets a total transferred budget of ≤ 12 MB on desktop and ≤ 6 MB on mobile — for the entire slice, not for one character. This one character is 12,391,432 bytes. That is 98.5 % of the desktop budget read as 12 MiB, or 103.3 % of it read as 12 MB at 10^6; the document does not say which unit it means, and the file lands either just inside or just outside depending on the reading. Against the mobile budget there is no ambiguity: it is 197 % of 6 MiB, or 206 % of 6 MB. Nothing else in the slice would fit alongside it on desktop, and it does not fit at all on mobile.

The GPU cost is separate from the transfer cost and was checked by arithmetic rather than restated. One 2048 × 2048 texture decompressed to RGBA8 is 2048 × 2048 × 4 = 16,777,216 bytes = 16 MiB at the base level. A full mip chain adds the geometric series 1/4 + 1/16 + …, which sums to 4/3 of the base level: 22,369,620 bytes = 21.333 MiB. Three such textures come to 67,108,860 bytes = **exactly 64 MiB**. The mobile tier's texture-memory budget in the same document is 128 MB in total, so this single character would occupy **50 %** of it, and 25 % of the desktop tier's 256 MB. Triangles are not the problem: 31,156 is 10.4 % of the mobile tier's ≤ 300 k budget. The textures are the whole of the cost.

**3. `doubleSided: true`.** The single material disables backface culling for the entire model, so every triangle is rasterised from both sides. Combined with the absent `alphaMode` the material is `OPAQUE`, so there is no transparency sorting problem, but the fragment cost of the model is up to twice what a single-sided material of the same geometry would be.

## Rule for future imports

Write the row first, then commit the file with it: name, creator, version, source URL, licence text location, verification date and method, every modification, and the category. If the primary source cannot be reached from the session, the asset waits.
