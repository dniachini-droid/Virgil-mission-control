# Product vision

Deliverable 3 (with `V1_BOUNDARY.md`). Source: master commission sections 1, 2 and 14; Amendment 1 section A. Written in the Cartographer's acceptance-contract shape so that the Phase 1 acceptance contract can be derived from it.

## Problem and intended user

Autonomous coding agents produce more work than one person can supervise through transcripts, chat windows and PR lists. The user is a single owner (later a small team) who commissions work across several repositories and needs trustworthy control over what was authorised, who did what, which SHA is which, what checks ran, whether review was independent, whether findings were reproduced and repaired, whether the reviewed artifact is still the current artifact, and when autonomy must stop.

## Product promise

Virgil Mission Control makes autonomous digital work visible, bounded and auditable in a navigable three-dimensional world where every consequential object is a fact with evidence. The artifact moves between workers; authority does not silently move with it. The owner alone merges.

## The two worlds

The Orbital Foundry shows operational truth as a psychedelic orbital foundry: stations, docking bays, specialist workers, sealed capsules with SHAs, transit corridors, scanner arrays, inspection stations, quarantine fields, repair docks, the owner airlock and the launch gate. The Mind of Virgil shows knowledge truth: sealed sources, compilation, durable structures, provenance tethers, contradictions, supersession and scan findings. They are co-equal, share one art direction and are connected by a causal gateway.

## What makes it different

- Evidence, not narrative: a builder's report is a claim; gates and independent review decide.
- Fifteen states that never collapse, rendered by form and label, not colour alone.
- Bounded repair: one cycle without the owner, two with; never a loop.
- A knowledge layer that compounds without absorbing live operational state.
- A renderer that cannot invent work: every authenticated animation needs its event and evidence.

## Long-term shape

A premium science-fiction strategy game's command layer fused with a rigorous SDLC platform: single project first (Phase 1), read-only real repositories (Phase 2), governed session launching (Phase 3), project creation (Phase 4), the multi-project universe with replay and the full Mind (Phase 5), hardening (Phase 6).

## Principles that constrain every phase

The universe may be surreal; the evidence must be exact. Workers may have personality; their authority must be bounded. Handoffs may be cinematic; the candidate SHA must be immutable. The Mind may feel alive; its sources, uncertainty and contradictions must remain visible. Knowledge may compound; live truth is never copied into stale memory. It may look like a game; it must govern real work like a serious system.
