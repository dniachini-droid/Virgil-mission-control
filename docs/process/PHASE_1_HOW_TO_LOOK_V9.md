# How to look — Phase 1, viewing point V9: a screen opens a panel with the full record; the ledger keeps the board; the visors read as black glass

**A new document rather than a note on V8's.** `PHASE_1_HOW_TO_LOOK_V8.md` already covers V8, V8.1, V8.2 and V8.3 in 220 lines and V9 adds a whole new surface; appending would have made one document nobody finishes. V8's stands unchanged as the record of what those passes asked you to look at — everything it describes is still true of this build — and this one covers only what is new.

**This pass answers eight things you said.** Clicking any screen now opens a window with the full record, and the camera goes to that station at the same moment rather than making you wait. Virgil's far-left slab is a ledger: a row per agent, its outcome, and how long it took, appended and never rewritten, so you can look away and still read where it is up to. All four visors read as black glass — the light-coloured marks in the middle are gone, and so are the indents Meshy left where it guessed the eyes were. Virgil's slab pictures fill their openings. `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE` no longer runs over the line beneath it. The honesty band is legible on a phone. And the centre slab reads black rather than olive during a verdict.

**Two things you asked for are not in this build**, and it is better you hear it here than find it: the screen turning **off** still cannot be reached from the demonstration, so nobody has yet seen the collapse in a frame; and the close-ups are still framed on the screen rather than on the character. Both are named in `PHASE_1_RUN_RECORD.md`, "V9 — items 4 and 5".

One file, download it, double-click it. It is **8.5 MB**, 0.4 % larger than V8.3.

> **What this viewing point asks:** does the panel look intentional and futuristic, and does it feel like it belongs to the world rather than to a web page; can you read where the run is up to from the ledger without watching it happen; and are the four faces clean black glass now?

### 3. Open it

Double-click. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari 14 or later. **The footer's stage line reads "viewing point V9"** — check that first.

### 4. The panel — the thing to spend your time on

**Click any screen.** A console's screen, or any one of Virgil's three slabs. Two things happen at once: a bright line draws down the right of the frame, the panel's ground opens out of it, and the record settles in — and *at the same time* the camera flies to that station underneath it. You are not waiting to be taken there before you can read.

- **It is a display surface, not a card.** The same near-black ink as the screens, hairline rules, the corner brackets the screens draw, no rounded card, no drop shadow, no grey browser scrollbar — a thin rail in the world's own ice instead. Nothing in it is a colour the world does not already use.
- **`ILLUSTRATIVE · NOT REAL STATE` is a solid amber band straight under the header**, at full width, on every panel. It is more prominent here than on the screens on purpose: full detail set in clean type is far more believable than a small glowing screen, so the marking matters more, not less.
- **The verdict's own ring is in the header**, the same twelve segments as on the screens — whole with a tick for a pass, broken with a cross for a block, outlined with a gap and an empty bracket for insufficient evidence, a thin open circle for the Fabricator's claim.
- **Scroll it.** The Fabricator's panel carries his plan, all eight files with their status and line counts, and his three commits. The Prover's carries all fourteen checks with their results. The Keeper's carries his findings with their severities and what the policy requires of a verdict.
- **The composer at the foot is real and typeable**, and it says what it is: *not connected to a session*, *nothing is sent*. Type in it — what you type stays. It never pretends to have sent anything, because there is nothing behind it yet.
- **`Esc`, or the control at the top right, dismisses it** — and leaves you at the station the camera flew to. It does not snap you back. `Esc` again returns to the wide view. One step per level.
- **On a phone** it is the whole screen, with the composer pinned at the bottom; drag it down to dismiss.
- **The keyboard reaches it too**: press `P` to open the panel for whatever you are looking at.

**What to tell me.** Does it look intentional? Does the opening read as one of the objects in the room switching on, or as a web panel sliding in? Is the type right? Is anything crowded?

### 5. The ledger — Virgil's far-left slab

**Press `5` for the Board**, or click the left slab. It is no longer a light that moves. It is a board:

- **`CANDIDATE 9abcdef012`** at the top, so a new run reads as a new run. It clears when a new candidate starts.
- **A row per agent, appended as each is given work and never rewritten.** `F`, `P`, `K` in a heavy box; the role's name; a bar whose length is how long the hop took; the seconds; and the verdict's own ring in its own colour.
- **A row in flight reads as unresolved** — the ring breathes open, the bar has a bright moving head, the seconds count up — never as blank.
- **When a verdict comes back it lands in its row**: the same convergence you see on the console seals the ring on the board, so the beat and the record are one event.
- **Click a row** and the panel opens on that hop.
- A blocked or an insufficient-evidence candidate never reaches review, so on those loops the board has two rows and not an empty third.

**Judged honestly, and you should check whether you agree:** at the wide view you can read *three hops, all green, all the same length, all finished* — the colour, the bar and the ring carry that far. **The role names do not read at the wide view**; you have to come closer or open the panel. That is the design (shape and colour at distance, text up close) but it is also the limit, and if you want the names legible from where you sit the slab has to get bigger or the rows fewer.

### 6. The faces

**Press `1` for Virgil.** The bright mark in the middle of his visor is gone. It was his face's own light, which sat in front of the glass and was being reflected straight back at you — it now sits behind the display where the glass cannot see it. There is still a faint travelling colour from the room on the glass; that is the reflection, and it is meant to be there.

**Press `2`, `3`, `4`** and look at each visor at the wide view too. The indents where Meshy guessed the eyes were — a mirrored pair on the Fabricator, 9.4 and 7.5 mm deep, and another on the Keeper, 13.6 and 12.6 mm — are filled, against the shape of the surface around them and never against a sphere. **Your models on disk are untouched**: nothing in `assets/` changed, and the smoothing happens when the visor is built.

**One thing left alone, and I want you to look at it and say.** The Keeper has a third concave region, 30 mm deep across 48,000 mm² in the middle of his hood. Filling everything concave was tried and measured and it made him *worse* — his surface went from 49 % convex to 42 % and his worst wrong-way curvature nearly doubled. So the fill stops at 15 mm deep and 8,000 mm² across, and that fold is left as your design. If it is Meshy's and not yours, say so and it can go the same way as the others.

### 7. The small ones

- **The Prover's `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`** (third loop, press `3`) no longer runs over the line beneath it.
- **On a phone at the wide view** the slabs' amber bands are now on two lines. All four words are still there — nothing is abbreviated or dropped — but each character gets about twice the width. Tell me whether that is enough or whether they need to be bigger still.
- **Virgil's slab pictures fill their openings.** A fifth of the honesty band used to be behind the bezel; it is now 4 %. What is still below the band is the case itself.
- **The `SCRIPTED DEMONSTRATION` badge has moved to the bottom left**, because at the top left it was sitting exactly on the ledger.

## What is still not measured, and is not claimed

Nothing in this build has been looked at on real graphics hardware, and no frame time has been taken anywhere. The two graphics-hardware checks in `OD-0005` are still **deferred and recorded as not performed, never as met**. The panel adds a layer of ordinary web page over the 3D scene and the visors carry 43,470 more triangles than they did before V8.3; neither has been measured. Your machine is the only display this project has.

Everything on every screen and in every panel is **illustrative**. No event, no check and no review drives any of it; the numbers are fixed schedules. Every surface says so.
