# Owner grant, 9 September 2026 — the Keeper now, and the following phases unattended

The owner, going to sleep, asked: *"Can I give you authority to run keeper straight away in a new
window and then continue with the subsequent phases without my go ahead"*, and this session accepted.
Recorded because a standing grant that lives only in a chat is the thing this project's own rules
exist to prevent.

## What is granted

1. **An independent Keeper review, started immediately**, in its own session, read-only, over the V11
   branch. It reviews an exact immutable candidate SHA and returns one of the four verdicts.
2. **Continuing the following phases unattended** — repairing what the Keeper finds, and carrying on
   with the work already agreed — without waiting for the owner's go-ahead on each step.

## What is not granted, and is not assumed

The hard limits in `CLAUDE.md` are untouched by this and continue to bind. In particular, and for the
avoidance of any doubt overnight:

- **No merge to `main`, no pull request, no deploy, no credentials.** Merge remains the owner's alone
  in every phase, as the product itself now states in its own interface.
- **No other repository.** His question earlier about pointing this interface at a different
  repository stays where it was recorded: unstarted, and needing its own written authorisation.
- **No change to the approved default version.** V10 stays reachable and unchanged; V11 does not
  become the default without the owner saying so.
- **No change to `constitution/` or `docs/product/VIRGIL_MASTER_COMMISSION.md`**, which only the owner
  may change.

## The boundary this session will hold on its own judgment

Work that is **objectively decidable** proceeds unattended: the Keeper's findings, correctness,
verification, measurement, performance, accessibility, and anything with a number attached.

Work that is a **matter of the owner's taste** is queued for him rather than guessed. Nearly every
visual improvement in the last day came from his eye on a rendered frame — the crooked screens, the
milky glass, the eye indents, the light marks, the slab gap, the camera height, the triangle. A
session that invents those choices overnight is likelier to drift than to converge, and would hand him
a pile of decisions already made. So: findings fixed, judgments listed.
